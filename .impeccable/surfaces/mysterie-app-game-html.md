# Surface: case player (mysterie-app/game.html)

Mode: Experience — the player is inside the case; the phone leads from the first viewport.
Audience/job: adults solving a case solo, 10–20 min, phone or desktop. Task: search the phone, pin evidence, answer five questions, accuse.
Must stay: realistic smartphone UI and app behaviour; phone beside the dossier (desktop) / dossier as sheet (mobile); all Dutch copy, story and clues.
Avoid: cartoonish or gamey (bright colours, badges, confetti). Also no horror, neon or crime-tape clichés.
Memorable moment: picking up the phone.
Build path: code-led (no image generation in session).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every case is an exhibition of a life after closing time. The dead man's phone lies on a plinth under one spotlight in a dark gallery; the player is the only visitor. Refuses the category default of manila folders, typewriter type and crime tape.

OWN-WORLD: Dark painted museum walls (deep green-black slate), one warm tungsten light pool, plaster-white plinth, exhibition wall labels (small precise grotesk, title/medium/date/collection lines), large wall-vinyl lettering for case titles, a single red "sold" dot as the only saturated colour, meaning suspect/pinned. Dossier = printed exhibition guide on the wall: label cards, hairline rules, catalogue numbering.

STORY: The player understands they are holding evidence, believes every object can speak, and acts by picking up the phone, then marking what is suspect with red dots until the case can be named.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height dark wall. Centre-left: the phone lying on a white plinth inside a soft spotlight cone from above. Right of it, at eye height: wall label "De Reiger" in vinyl lettering with object label lines (Telefoon, zwart · gevonden naast het lichaam · vrijdag 14 november · collectie Recherche Haarlem), the content note, the three moves, and the primary action "Pak de telefoon". Mobile: the label stacks above the plinth.

FORM: Night gallery, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list (1 only light, 2 evidence bag, 3 proces-verbaal, 4 wet polder night, 5 night gallery, 6 mortuary, 7 interrogation mirror). seed:ddd7ad00b95b4ee4. Signature interaction: tap "Pak de telefoon", the phone lifts off the plinth and turns upright, the spotlight dims to the screen's glow and the lock screen wakes. Motion grammar: slow, weighted, exponential ease-out; light changes rather than bouncing things.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
